# Adversarial Seam Review — [Proyecto Auto] Architecture Spine

## Overall verdict

The spine's ten ADs are individually well-formed and each closes the seam it names, but they were written as **isolated point-fixes**, not as a system: several genuinely load-bearing facts (which brands an agency carries, whether a saved row is mutable after sharing, what "left the catalog" means, what the filter/search contract actually looks like on the wire) have no AD at all, or are covered by an AD whose language is precise about one half of the problem and silent about the other. Two teams — or even one team building two endpoints six weeks apart — can each read every AD literally, ship code that individually satisfies it, and still produce a backend/frontend pair that disagrees about the shape of the truth. Ten concrete findings below; the two marked critical (AGENCY_BRAND dual-sourcing, and saved-comparison mutability vs. the "share links never change" guarantee) are the ones most likely to actually ship broken because nothing in the spine even gestures at them.

## Findings

- **critical** Two independently-buildable sources of "which brands does this agency carry": the ERD's `AGENCY_BRAND` join (`AGENCY ||--o{ AGENCY_BRAND`, `BRAND ||--o{ AGENCY_BRAND`) and the transitive fact via `AGENCY ||--o{ VEHICLE`/`BRAND ||--o{ VEHICLE`. A backoffice "upload vehicles for Agency X" module (FR-14, ≤30-min acceptance target invites shortcuts) can create `Vehicle` rows for a new Brand at that Agency without anyone touching `AGENCY_BRAND`, or vice versa curate an `AGENCY_BRAND` row for a brand with zero vehicles yet. Both modules honor the ERD and every AD to the letter — no AD mentions `AGENCY_BRAND` at all (the "Load-bearing invariants" list under the ERD skips it entirely) — yet FR-2's "agency profile shows brands carried" and FR-3's "list vehicles by agency" can now tell two different stories for the same agency. (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md, ERD mermaid block + "Load-bearing invariants on these entities" list.) *Fix:* new AD naming `AGENCY_BRAND` as either (a) the single curated source of truth with `Vehicle.Brand` validated to be a subset of it at write time, or (b) a derived/generated set (view or trigger-maintained) from `Vehicle`, with `AGENCY_BRAND` as a table removed from hand-curation entirely. Pick one; today both are live options.

- **critical** AD-10 fixes what a *read* of a saved/shared comparison renders (the frozen snapshot) but says nothing about whether a `SavedComparison` row is mutable after it has been shared. EXPERIENCE.md's `comparison-table` behavior ("la acción de quitar... misma regla que en escritorio... la comparación se recalcula al quitar un vehículo sin salir de la pantalla") applies generally to the Comparación (tabla) surface, including when reached via "Mis comparaciones guardadas" — it is only carved out for the read-only public view. A "view/edit my saved comparison" handler built to honor that UX rule literally (PATCH removes a `ComparisonVehicle` row in place) and a "resolve share link" handler built to honor AD-7/AD-10 literally (share token resolves to the same `SavedComparison` id, rendering whatever snapshot rows currently exist) combine into: owner removes a vehicle from their saved comparison after already sharing it, and everyone holding that "never expires" link now silently sees a 2-vehicle comparison instead of the 3-vehicle one they were sent — with no AD forbidding it and no AD requiring copy-on-write instead. (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md AD-10 and AD-7; EXPERIENCE.md `comparison-table` row and Interaction Primitives → "Compartir".) *Fix:* new AD: `SavedComparison`/`ComparisonVehicle` are immutable once a share token has been issued (or: immutable period, and "remove a vehicle" always creates a new `SavedComparison` + new share token, never mutates an existing shared one).

- **high** Nothing pins down the REST filter/search contract itself (query param names, enum value sets for body type/transmission/equipment level, casing, combination semantics for FR-4–FR-9's 7 filter dimensions). AD-2 says the two clients' "only shared contract... is the backend REST API" and AD-4 makes them "pure API clients," but neither AD — nor anything else in the spine — requires that contract to be specified anywhere (OpenAPI, shared DTO catalog, etc.) before both clients build against it. An Angular team building `GET /vehicles?bodyType=SUV&priceMax=500000` and a Flutter team independently building `GET /vehicles?carroceria=suv&precio_max=500000` each satisfy AD-2/AD-4 to the letter — "consume exclusively through REST contract" doesn't say the contract was ever written down anywhere both teams could converge on it. Only one shape can actually exist server-side; the other client silently breaks integration, and NFR-3 functional parity is asserted (per AD-2) "against the API contract, not each other" — but there's no artifact that is that contract. *Fix:* new AD requiring an OpenAPI (or equivalent) spec, generated/reviewed from the `Application` layer's DTOs, as the one artifact both clients build against — versioned, and named as binding on FR-4–FR-9 and FR-13/NFR-3.

- **high** AD-7 fixes the share token; AD-5 fixes owner-scoped *access*. Neither addresses **creation**: EXPERIENCE.md's "Compartir" is explicitly usable "con o sin sesión — no requiere guardar primero," meaning a `SavedComparison` (something must exist to carry a share token) can be created with no owner. A `POST /comparisons/share` endpoint and a `POST /comparisons` (Guardar) endpoint, built independently, each honor AD-5 (owner-scoped check simply doesn't fire for a null-owner row) and AD-7 (share token distinct from internal id) to the letter — but nothing says whether sharing-then-later-saving-the-same-selection reuses that row (attaches an owner to it) or always creates a second, duplicate `SavedComparison`. The likely outcome: an orphaned, ownerless row that AD-5's ownership check can never surface in "Mis comparaciones guardadas," permanently reachable only via its share link, with no owner and no lifecycle rule governing it. (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md AD-5, AD-7; EXPERIENCE.md Interaction Primitives → "Compartir".) *Fix:* new AD specifying whether "Guardar" after an anonymous "Compartir" claims/attaches ownership to the existing row (single row, one identity) vs. is defined as always creating a distinct owned copy — and whether ownerless rows are a permanent, intended state or need any retention rule.

- **high** AD-10 says the vehicle *reference* on `ComparisonVehicle` is "used only to check it still exists in the catalog" for share-link invalidation, and EXPERIENCE.md's public-share empty state fires "si el vehículo referenciado salió del catálogo." Neither defines what "left the catalog" means at the data level. Given the PRD's weekly-curated-catalog model (team manually maintains agencies/vehicles, not a live feed), a "weekly catalog refresh" backoffice module is far more likely to soft-delete/deactivate (`Vehicle.IsActive = false`, preserving history/FKs) than hard-delete rows — while a "resolve share link" query handler built independently, taking AD-10's "still exists" at face value, checks only row presence. Result: a discontinued vehicle a curator marked inactive weeks ago still resolves fine on every outstanding share link, showing stock/price data for a vehicle no agency sells anymore — directly undermining the PRD's "discrepancy reported" counter-metric. *Fix:* tighten AD-10 to define catalog existence explicitly as an active-status check (not row presence), binding both the backoffice deactivation path and the share-resolution path to the same predicate.

- **medium** FR-15 (binding PRD requirement, not flagged Open Question) includes phone-based account creation; EXPERIENCE.md's `account-form-field` literally offers "email o teléfono." The spine's Deferred list, however, leaves "SMS OTP channel — v1 or later... email is the default channel" open. A web team building `account-form-field` to the UX spec's letter ships a phone-entry option; a backend team building to the Deferred note's letter ships email-only OTP issuance. Neither violates any AD (AD-4 only requires *both clients* delegate to *the same* auth endpoints — it never says which identity channels those endpoints must support), yet phone signup either 500s, or one client (built by whoever read the Deferred note) quietly drops the phone option while the other doesn't — a direct NFR-3 parity break sourced entirely from a Deferred item that touches a hard FR. *Fix:* promote to an AD: MVP OTP channel is email-only, and `account-form-field`/its client-side spec is bound to that (drop phone entry, or gate it behind a "próximamente" state) until SMS ships — do not leave the identity-channel/OTP-channel mismatch to be discovered at integration time.

- **medium** AD-8's "enforced as a DB-level constraint/check" doesn't distinguish a Postgres `CHECK` (which cannot count sibling rows in `ComparisonVehicle` without a supporting column/trigger) from a trigger-based aggregate enforcement, and doesn't address concurrent writes. Two independently built write paths — e.g. the normal "Guardar comparación" command and a later "duplicate this comparison" or backoffice seeding utility — could each pass client-side/API validation (count ≤ 2 before insert) then both insert a 3rd/4th row in the same race window if the "DB-level" half is actually just a per-row `CHECK(ordinal <= 3)` rather than a true cross-row guard, silently defeating the cap AD-8 exists to protect. *Fix:* tighten AD-8 to name the actual mechanism (e.g., "enforced via a Postgres trigger raising on `COUNT(*) > 3` per `SavedComparisonId`, not a per-row CHECK"), so every write path is guaranteed to hit the same guard regardless of how it was built.

- **medium** AD-10 requires "a frozen snapshot of the comparable attributes (price, availability, etc.)" but never enumerates the field set beyond FR-10's minimum (price/year, body type, transmission/engine, equipment level). EXPERIENCE.md's mobile `comparison-table` card additionally shows "nombre, agencia" per vehicle. A "Create/Save comparison" command handler built strictly off FR-10's minimum won't snapshot agency name or color; a "Render saved/shared comparison" handler then either shows blank agency/color fields, or — worse — falls back to a live join against current `Vehicle`/`Agency` data for the fields that weren't snapshotted, which directly violates AD-10's "never a live join to current vehicle data." Two handlers, each locally consistent with AD-10's text, produce either broken UI or a silent AD-10 violation depending on which one shipped first. *Fix:* tighten AD-10 with an explicit, complete field list for the snapshot (matching exactly what `comparison-table` renders per EXPERIENCE.md, including agency name/color), so no renderer is ever tempted to join live.

- **low** AD-9 requires a "server-set `last_updated`... derived by the client at render time," but doesn't say whether that timestamp is stamped per-record-on-change (only vehicles actually edited in a weekly batch get a new value) or per-batch-on-run (every vehicle in the weekly refresh gets touched/re-stamped, changed or not). A backoffice bulk-upload endpoint built to only touch changed rows and a "weekly refresh job" built to re-stamp everything it processes are both "server-set... AD-9-compliant," but produce very different "actualizado hace N días" microcopy for the same vehicle depending on which path last wrote it — undermining the PRD's freshness-discrepancy counter-metric in a subtler way than #5 above. *Fix:* tighten AD-9 to specify stamping semantics (e.g., "`last_updated` reflects the most recent weekly catalog run in which this vehicle's agency was processed, regardless of whether individual fields changed").

- **low** AD-6 fixes geography as structured fields *on `Agency`*, but NFR-2 (<2s search/filter, including implied location-based vehicle search per Flujo 1's "filtrando por agencias presentes en su estado/municipio") creates real pressure to denormalize city/state onto `Vehicle` for query performance, since every vehicle search that filters by location would otherwise require a join to `Agency` per request. AD-6 doesn't forbid or govern such a copy, so a "vehicle search" endpoint optimized for NFR-2 could introduce a second, unsynced geography field on `Vehicle` — a second owner of "where is this" alongside `Agency.city/state/country`, with no AD requiring it to stay in sync when an agency's location is corrected. *Fix:* either explicitly forbid denormalizing geography onto `Vehicle` (always join/filter via `Agency`) or, if performance requires a copy, mandate it as a trigger/view-maintained derivative of `Agency`, never independently writable.
